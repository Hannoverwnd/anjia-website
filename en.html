WHY Chuanggao Artisan

Why Choose Chuanggao Artisan

It is the details you do not see that make windows and doors reliable for years. Four core safeguards protect the quiet and comfort of every home.

Thermal Efficiency

Multi-chamber thermal-break aluminum profiles + insulating Low-E glass help retain heat in winter and block heat in summer, reducing annual heating and cooling costs.

Sound Insulation

Triple sealing gaskets combined with insulating glass significantly reduce outdoor noise, helping homes near busy roads stay quieter.

Premium Hardware

Multi-point locking hardware provides smooth operation and reliable anti-pry protection, maintaining a smooth feel through more than 100,000 opening cycles.

Precision Manufacturing

Standardized production includes corner sealing, reinforced corner joints and individual testing of sealing and operation before shipment to prevent water and air leakage.

OUR STORY

From a small workshop to a smart factory with 100,000 m² annual capacity

In 2010, Chuanggao Artisan Doors & Windows started as a small workshop. From contract manufacturing to building our own brand, we have followed one simple principle: get the unseen details right — profiles, glass, hardware and workmanship — and a strong reputation follows naturally.

Today, Chuanggao Artisan operates its own factory and standardized production lines. Our portfolio covers residential windows and doors, sunrooms and curtain-wall projects, with solutions delivered to owners across more than 300 residential developments nationwide.
